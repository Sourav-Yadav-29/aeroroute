Capabilities

Everything a mission needs.

From interactive graph editing to animated flight visualization — combining a Python graph algorithm backend with a zero-dependency web interface.

Interactive flight network

Click to place stops, toggle corridor availability. The weighted undirected graph updates live as you edit.

Real-time route planning

Python backend runs Dijkstra’s algorithm on every replan. Shortest paths, intermediate vertices, and predecessor maps — all exposed.

Battery-aware scheduling

Every candidate is checked against energy needed to reach it and return safely. No candidate is allowed to strand the drone.

Priority delivery tiers

High, Normal, and Low tiers. Greedy nearest-neighbour picks the most urgent feasible stop first. IDs break ties deterministically.

Under the hood

Three ideas.
One optimal mission.

AeroRoute chains three classic algorithms, each solving a different part of the delivery problem.

01

Dijkstra’s algorithm

A heapq min-priority queue finds the shortest path between every pair of locations — including intermediate vertices the drone must fly through.

02

Priority-aware nearest neighbour

Among feasible stops, pick the highest-priority tier first, then the nearest stop within that tier. Toggle priority mode off for pure nearest-neighbour.

03

Battery-constrained feasibility

Before any stop is committed, the planner verifies enough energy remains to reach it and fly back. Infeasible stops are deferred, not dropped.

Energy model

The maths behind the mission.

Usable energy is always clamped to zero

If charge falls below the reserve, the difference is discarded — the drone never plans to use power it doesn’t have.

Reserve is based on full capacity

The safety reserve is applied to the battery’s total capacity, not the starting charge — making it a reliable absolute floor.

Service time is separate from energy

Delivery service time adds to mission duration but consumes no energy — keeping the planner fast and predictable.

# Initial charge available initial = capacity_Wh × charge_pct / 100 # Minimum energy kept in reserve reserve = capacity_Wh × reserve_pct / 100 # What the drone can actually use usable = max(0, initial − reserve) # Energy consumed on a flight leg consumed = distance_km × Wh_per_km # Total mission time (minutes) duration = distance / speed × 60 + stops × service_min
Scope & deployment

Educational by design.
Extendable by intent.

AeroRoute is a classroom and portfolio project. It deliberately omits real-world concerns to keep the algorithm logic clear and inspectable.

What’s included

Weighted graph routing, Dijkstra’s algorithm, priority scheduling, battery constraints, animated simulation, scenario persistence, responsive UI — all in one package.

What’s omitted

Weather, terrain, elevation, payload, real airspace restrictions, hardware integration, authentication, multi-user access, or production hosting configuration.

Running locally

Start with python run.py --port 8080 (or npm start). Open http://127.0.0.1:8080.
